\section*{Part A: Cover letter feedback}
{\small\itshape\color{GTGray}Read your partner's letter next to the posting. For a personal statement, read ``posting'' as ``program.''}

\prompt{1.\quad Does the opening name the job and give one reason to keep reading?}
{Underline the sentence that says which position this is. If no sentence does, say so.}
\lines{2}

\prompt{2.\quad Which qualification is best supported, and which needs evidence?}
{Name it, show it, size it, connect it. Point to one sentence that needs an example or a number.}
\lines{2}

\prompt{3.\quad Could this letter be sent unchanged to another employer?}
{Suggest one specific detail about this employer or program to add.}
\lines{2}

\section*{Part B: Mock interview}
{\small\itshape\color{GTGray}You are the interviewer. Ask in order, don't interrupt, take notes. Then swap roles.}

\prompt{1.\quad ``Tell me about yourself.''}
{Present, past, future in 60--90 seconds. Did it sound like the letter's opening, said out loud?}
\lines{2}

\prompt{2.\quad ``Tell me about a time you solved a hard technical problem.''}
{Situation, Task, Action, Result.}
\lines{2}

\prompt{3.\quad ``Why do you want this position, and why here?''}
{Did your partner name something specific about the employer or program? Did it match the letter?}
\lines{2}
